Privacy & cookies.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Harbour Town is responsible for the personal information described on this page. You can contact us at harbourtownuk@gmail.com about privacy questions or requests.

When you contact us

If you email us about a gig, booking or another enquiry, we receive the details you send, such as your name, email address and message. We use them to reply and manage the enquiry or booking. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and arranging performances; where we agree a booking with you, we may also need the correspondence to perform that agreement. Please avoid sending sensitive information that we do not need.

We keep correspondence while it is needed to handle your enquiry, any booking and reasonable follow-up, then review and delete it when no longer needed. If legal or accounting obligations apply to a booking, relevant records may be kept for longer. If you contact us through Facebook, Meta also handles information under its own privacy policy.

Visits to this website

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, which can include IP address, device and browser details, pages requested and security events. We use this service to run the website securely, relying on our legitimate interest in operating and protecting it. Cloudflare may use strictly necessary security technologies. We do not currently run our own advertising or analytics tracking scripts.

The site loads its Inter typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to retrieve the font. Other external sites linked from here, including Bandcamp and Facebook, handle data under their own policies when you follow those links.

Music and videos

Bandcamp and YouTube players only connect when you choose “Load” on a player. Loading one sends information about your visit to that provider and may allow it to use cookies or similar technologies under its own policy. You can use the direct Bandcamp links without loading the embedded player on our site. See Bandcamp’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy for details. Your choice applies to that player for the current page view; we do not store it.

Cookies

The public pages do not set Harbour Town analytics or advertising cookies. The private gig editor uses a strictly necessary cookie called ht_gig_session for authorised sign-in. It lasts up to eight hours and is removed on sign-out. This cookie is not used to track public visitors. Our hosting provider may use necessary security cookies. Optional third-party player cookies are only possible after you choose to load a player, as described above.

Sharing and international services

We use Cloudflare to host and protect the website, Google for the font, and email services to receive and answer messages. Bandcamp, YouTube and Meta process data when you choose their players or links. Some of these providers may process information outside the UK. Their own privacy information explains where they process it and the safeguards they use.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask us for access to your personal information, correction or deletion, or object to or restrict how we use it. Email harbourtownuk@gmail.com and we will respond to your request. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

We will update this page if the site’s services or data practices change.
