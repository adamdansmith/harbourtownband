Privacy & cookies.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Harbour Town uses this site to share music and gig information. If you email us, we use the details you send to reply and arrange any booking. We keep correspondence only while it is needed for the enquiry, booking or related records. For a question or request about your information, email harbourtownuk@gmail.com. You can also raise a concern with the ICO.

Cookies and media

Cloudflare hosts and protects the site and may use necessary security technologies. The private gig editor uses an essential sign-in cookie (ht_gig_session) lasting up to eight hours. We do not run advertising or analytics cookies of our own. Your cookie choice is saved on this device so we can remember it.

With your permission, embedded Bandcamp and YouTube players load on our pages. These services receive information about your visit and may use their own cookies. If you choose essential only, the players stay off until you change your choice. Read Bandcamp’s policy and Google’s policy for their details.

The site also loads a font from Google Fonts. Following an external link, such as Facebook or Bandcamp, takes you to that service’s own privacy policy.
