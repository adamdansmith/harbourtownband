01 / The music

South Parade

The latest album from Harbour Town.

Eleven original songs recorded in Portsmouth and Southsea. The album takes its name from the seafront, with stories, voices and arrangements drawn from the seven musicians who made it.

02 / The band

From Scarlet Town to Harbour Town

Harbour Town formed in 2021 from its sister group Scarlet Town. Our musicians bring decades of playing and recording experience to original songs influenced by British and American singer-songwriters and roots music.

Voices, guitar, double bass, drums, saxophones, clarinet, strings and flute all have a place in the sound.
