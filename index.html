ABOUT

Meet
the band.

Harbour Town was formed in 2021 from its sister group Scarlet Town.

These musicians have vast experience gained over decades of playing and recording. The range of instrumentation provides the band with the opportunity to generate exciting and varied arrangements to supplement the original song writing. The music reflects a range of British and American influences from the past 60 years, including singer-songwriters and roots music, expressed through a unique combination of instrumentation and vocalists.

The resultant material reflects the talent, diversity and richness that is: Harbour Town.
