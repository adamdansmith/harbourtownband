About the band

Meet Harbour Town

Seven musicians from Portsmouth, bringing a wide range of instruments and influences to original songs.

Our story

Hearing is believing.

Harbour Town was formed in 2021 from its sister group Scarlet Town. Between us, we have decades of experience playing and recording.

Our mix of instruments gives us room to build varied arrangements around the original songwriting. The music reflects British and American influences from the past 60 years, especially singer-songwriters and roots music.

The people

The band

Denis Reeve-Baker

Guitar & vocals

Den is the songwriter for the band. He has played in Reet Petite and Gone, The Red Sky Divers and Scarlet Town.

Jo Alldridge

Vocals & shaker

Jo comes from a musical theatre background and has performed at the Kings Theatre in Southsea with the Portsmouth Players.

Dave Jordan

Double bass & backing vocals

Dave has played with the Polite Mechanicals and Scarlet Town, and ran the Barebones acoustic music venue and Mayfly festival events in Portsmouth.

Paul Hookham

Drums & percussion

Paul played with Scarlet Town and with Den in The Operation. His experience also includes The English Subtitles, The Woodentops and The Redskins.

Sue “Scooby” Dobbyn

Saxophones, clarinet, cello, violin & vocals

Scooby has played with Den in Ferris Wheel, The Red Sky Divers and Scarlet Town. She arranges the band’s wind, brass and string parts.

Adam Smith

Saxophones, clarinet & flute

A music graduate of Royal Holloway, Adam also played in The Red Sky Divers and Scarlet Town with Scooby and Den.

Sue Ryder-Morgan

Flute

Sue first played in a band with Den in 1978, and was later a member of The Red Sky Divers and Scarlet Town.
